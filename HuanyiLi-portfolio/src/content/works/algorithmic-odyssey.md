## A system you can play

A physical board game inspired by Monopoly, exploring algorithmic bias through identities, choices, and unequal opportunities.

![Algorithmic Odyssey — physical board game](/assets/cover-board.jpg)

### Letting rules carry meaning

Players draw role cards and navigate bias, job, and opportunity tiles. Character abilities, money, and Mental Health Points shape each journey. After five laps, the factions compare their combined Mental Health Points.

The board, cards, and physical model belong to this standalone tabletop project.
