## What does it mean to rise?

A Unity game that explores power and recurring social hierarchies through spatial progression, tasks, and a final choice.

![Final outcome — ascending](/assets/cover-ascending.jpg)

### Building a spatial metaphor

Inspired by the narrative structures of Animal Farm and Snowpiercer, the game organizes a journey through a farm, a circus, and higher levels of an animal society.

### Turning a world into actions

Collecting objects, talking to characters, finding mechanisms, and combat create an interaction framework. Players learn the world’s rules through action and gradually encounter the consequences of their choices.

### Returning to the central question

At the top, players face a choice between becoming a ruler and breaking the system. Levels, interfaces, and tasks ask whether moving upward necessarily means creating change.
