## An ocean seen through a little fish

An exhibition concept that turns coral bleaching into a journey visitors can explore and participate in.

![Final outcome — coral](/assets/cover-coral.jpg)

### Noticing what gets overlooked

Aquarium observations suggested that colorful fish attracted attention while coral information panels were often overlooked. The project asks how ecological information can become an engaging experience.

### Building an exploratory journey

A small fish’s perspective connects coral, ocean conditions, and habitats. A multi-entry maze, educational content, animation, and participatory moments organize the visitor journey.

### My contribution

In this collaborative project, I contributed research, designed the 2.5D maze, and edited animation. Spatial layout and sequence help visitors discover information gradually.
