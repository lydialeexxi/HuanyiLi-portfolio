## When the light begins to fade

A virtual experience exploring the relationship between changing vision, emotion, and adaptation.

![Final outcome — fading-light](/assets/cover-fading.jpg)

### Starting with perception

The project considers the uncertainty of losing familiar visual cues. Research, interview questions, and visual experiments explore how an experience can communicate that uncertainty.

### Letting space tell the story

Different environments, lighting conditions, and fields of visibility structure the experience. Participants encounter change through movement and exploration.

### Designing with care

This is a creative exploration of perception and empathy. As visual cues become less available, the design asks how guidance can remain understandable and useful.
