## Time has a rhythm of its own

**MFA Year 1 · Semester 1 — Major Studio project.**

A pulse-responsive digital hourglass that places measurable time alongside the body’s experience of time.

### Making a feeling visible

A physical hourglass represents measurable time, while a digital hourglass responds to physiological signals. The installation uses this contrast to make subjective time perceptible.

### Connecting input and feedback

A pulse sensor and Arduino connect bodily signals to real-time visuals in TouchDesigner. The heartbeat becomes an input to the experience rather than an isolated measurement.

### Learning through prototyping

Particle and liquid behaviors remained technical challenges. The experiment emphasized that sensors need a clear conceptual role and feedback that participants can understand.
