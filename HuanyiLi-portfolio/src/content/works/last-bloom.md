## Collapse is where the game begins

**MFA Year 1 · Semester 2 — Major Studio project.**

A first-person VR escape-room experience about the small, unresolved pressures that accumulate before a breaking point. An overgrown inner world turns an invisible emotional process into a space the player can explore.

![The Last Bloom — final immersive environment](/assets/last-bloom-world.png)

### Zone A / Read the signals

Text, illustrations, flowers, and a keypad become clues. The first zone introduces ignored stress through an environmental puzzle, asking the player to observe and connect what they find.

![Zone A — environmental clues and flower puzzle](/assets/bloom-zone-a.png)

### Zone B / Face the burden

The route moves from subtle signals to personal stress. Embodied actions — reaching, carrying, and confronting — give the escape-room structure a physical rhythm inside VR.

![Zone B — final in-world scene](/assets/bloom-zone-b.png)

### Zone C / Restore balance

Inner distortion becomes part of the puzzle space. The balance-scale interaction makes weight and release tangible as the player moves toward the final encounter.

![Final balance-scale interaction](/assets/bloom-balance.png)

### The final encounter

Unresolved pressures take the form of a creature: the visible “last straw” after many smaller burdens. The final stage frames release as a return from the inner world.
