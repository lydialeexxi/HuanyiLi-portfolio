## If memories could be rewritten

A speculative concept exploring memory, emotion, and technological intervention through a future VR and brain–computer interface scenario.

![Final outcome — mirage](/assets/cover-mirage.jpg)

### Posing a future question

If technology could change memories, how might people relate to pain and reality? This question establishes a fictional future scenario rather than claiming a currently available capability.

### Giving the scenario structure

Futures cones, futures wheels, and user flows explore device forms, service relationships, and situations of use, making an abstract hypothesis more tangible.

### Opening a conversation

The device and experience concept invites discussion about emotion, agency, and technological dependence. It is speculative design, not an implemented brain–computer system.
