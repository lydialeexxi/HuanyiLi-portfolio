## A clearer path to a fresh start

An employment-support platform concept for formerly incarcerated people, connecting job opportunities, skills development, and community support.

![Final outcome — renewpath](/assets/cover-renewpath.jpg)

### Understanding the challenge

Re-entering society involves more than finding a job. Employment discrimination, skills gaps, and limited support networks often overlap. ReNewPath addresses these connected barriers as a service-design problem.

### Turning needs into a journey

Background research, interviews, and personas informed a shared information architecture for job searches, conversations, training, and support services. Journey mapping helped identify where users need a clearer next step.

### Making the next step visible

The concept connects job listings and details with recruiter communication, skills records, and feedback. The work documents a process from research to high-fidelity interfaces; it is a design concept rather than a launched product.
